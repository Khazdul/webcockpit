Notes

  • One choice at a time. The check looks like a checkbox but works as a radio: checking a card unchecks the others.
  • Credit and season sit right of the Tileset label, as the dropdown's help line does today.
  • Light sets (Gefe & Rik, Gray's Map) still switch the background to White, and leaving them restores the colour you had.
  • Order. Background colour and Map file come before the tilesets, so the grid ends the page.
  • Same height as today. Appearance is the tallest tab (532 px of content), so the popup is 599 px tall. Mapper is 293 px today. Five per row at 12 px tiles (72 px previews) makes it about 495 px, so the popup does not grow and no tab gets more room. The panel above is drawn at today's body height.
  • Rows. Shimrod and its four seasons fill the first row; Default, Desert, Gefe & Rik and Gray's Map the second.
  • The check moves to the preview's corner so the name gets the card's full width. The alternating card is just "Shimrod": its four corners and the "now Autumn" line say the rest.
  • Map pane gear popup (403 px wide) keeps five columns; the tiles shrink to about 10 px.
