# Round 2 — the ten variants

Each variant is `round2/NN-name.html`: a copy of `round2/base.html` (read
its "How to make a variant" header) where only SCENE, LIFE (at least the
leaves) and the SELECTION STYLE block are replaced. Menu, wordmark, sky,
weather director, profile page and demo panel stay as in the base (fix a
base bug if you hit one, and say so). Every variant has the distant,
subtle, ORIGINAL magic gate in the mountain (BRIEF-round2 §Landscape) unless
noted; it glows a little at night, more with a bright moon, and is only a
hint by day. Tree tops sway in the wind (env.wind / env.swayAt).
