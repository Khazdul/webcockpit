# Round 3 — five sea proposals

Each is `round3/NN-name.html`, a copy of `round3/base.html` with the SCENE
(and LIFE where needed) replaced. Wordmark, panel, sky, weather director,
profile and demo stay as in the base. Shared rules: BRIEF-round3.md (no
mountains, no heightfields, one smooth rendering language, only clumped
tree tops, never lone or full-height trees, a simple distant cottage roof
with smoke, a slow distant original elven ship).

| # | File | Idea |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | `01-coast-wood.html` | **Kustskogen** — closest to the owner's words. Horizon ≈ 45 % from top. Dense dark conifer tops in big clumps lower left and right, smaller mixed tops in the middle. Cottage roof right of centre. Calm sea, long glitter path. |
| 2 | `02-soft-evening.html` | **Mjukt kvällsljus** — the same elements painted soft: no hard edges anywhere, far layers blurred with atmosphere, a low horizon (≈ 60 % from top) so the sky is large, mist on the water, tree tops as soft dark masses with only the nearest tips crisp. |
| 3 | `03-bay.html` | **Viken** — a bay: two low, hazy headlands (simple soft silhouettes, no terrain detail) narrow the sea toward the horizon; the ship sails out of the bay toward the west; the cottage roof on the left among birches and pines. |
| 4 | `04-high-view.html` | **Högt ovan** — a higher vantage: horizon high (≈ 33 % from top), lots of sea with slow swells, cloud shadows and sky reflections; a thick band of tree tops at the bottom with a gap in the middle; the ship tiny with a faint wake; gulls below us. |
| 5 | `05-silver-coast.html` | **Silverkusten** — moonlight first: a cooler palette, the sea's silver path the main event at night, stars mirrored on calm nights, the ship's small lantern; the trees frame the view like a vignette around the bottom and lower corners; by day pale sail, white foam lines far out. |
