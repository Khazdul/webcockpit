   what they are (twinkling/flaring stars, sky-mirror artefacts, moon halo,

   forest**, not across the sky.
4. **The yellow/red broadleaf crowns (and blossom puffs) are too big and
   look strange.** Remove them from the forest: **otherwise only conifer
   forest.** Instead, in the more distant part near the cottage, a **glade
   or grove with grass** where a few flowers or leaves in those colours
   show (by season). The glade and flowers are **blurred** so they melt in,
   **sparse**, **partly covered by trees**, not sharply defined.
5. **Sunrise too fast** right at the horizon. The sun should **rise
   slowly**, starting **just off-screen to the left** and gliding into view
   from the left as it climbs (a low, slow, shallow start of the arc). Make
   sunset the mirror of that (slow, sinking out to the right) unless it
   looks wrong; the moon may follow the same kind of path.

   right** (blue outline in `owner/shore-and-house.png`). **Only the lower
   left keeps the near, sharp trees** as they are now. The right side has
   only **more distant forest**. The **shoreline** runs roughly like the
   red line in that shot: from about the lower middle rising gently to the
   right edge (land below the line, sea above).
7. **The cottage moves onto land**, down the slope in the forest on the
   right (sketched in blue in the same shot), **more distant, blurred and
   partly hidden by trees** (today it looks as if it sits in the water).
   **Night: one window lit and smoke from the chimney. Day: no lit window
   and no smoke.**
8. **No lantern in the hood plate either** (owner, later): the glossy plate
