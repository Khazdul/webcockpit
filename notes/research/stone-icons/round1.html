Stone icons

Five proposals for every stone the Character card can show, and for a generic stone per group. Each is drawn as the card draws it: one colour, a line icon on the stone, the time bar under it. Shown at the card's sizes (13 px; 10 px in low panes) and enlarged four times.

How to answer. Name a stone and a number, e.g. blindness 3, or click proposals: your picks collect in the box at the bottom and in the sample row.
Colours are the Timers groups' own: buffs green, debuffs red, herblores leaf green, sanctuary pink; flags take the debuffs' red (ADR 0166 point 130).
Pairs. Stones that belong together use the same number for the same idea (comfortable 2 and very comfortable 2; dark aura and dark aura (faded); heightened senses and its faded form), so one answer can cover both.
Flags and twins. blind, hungry and thirsty are one stone with blindness, hunger and thirst (point 128); they are drawn once, under Flags. Proposal 1 of each flag and of sanctuary is today's icon.
New marks a stone the app does not recognise yet (see inventory.md): its icon is for when the stone is added.
Dashed stones are untimed (no end known), as on the card; the others show a part-drained bar.
