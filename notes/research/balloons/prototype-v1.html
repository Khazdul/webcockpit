Balloon Goblin

Prototype of balloon notices for kills. Press a button: the goblin rises from above the prompt, drifts, shows the kill and the XP, and leaves quickly over the top. Kills that come while a balloon is still up go on the same sign.

The six faces

  • The face follows the Statistics pane's rating: the kill's XP against the median of your last 30 kills. A burst shows the best face of the kills on the sign.
  • The goblin is drawn in SVG, so seasonal clothes (a winter hood, sunglasses) or another passenger for evil races are new layers, not new pictures.
  • In the client it would be a switch under Settings → Weather effects, beside Body.
