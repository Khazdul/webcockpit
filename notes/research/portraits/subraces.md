## Free Peoples

- **Men**: Dúnadan (Dúnedain), Eriadorian (also Eriadoran), Rohirrim,
  Beorning, and a Man of no kind.
  - Dúnadan (T): tall, grey-eyed, dark-haired. Bonus: detect evil, blades,
    leadership, tracking.
  - Eriadorian: "common people of northwestern Middle-earth", villages,
    Bree the best known. Bonus: cheaper rent, practice points.
  - Rohirrim: "tall, blond-haired, and often blue-eyed", stern, fierce,
    generous; horse people of Edoras. Bonus: riding.
  - Beorning: "fierce Men" of the Vale of Anduin, said to descend from
    great bears; change into a Bear (seen by others as "a Bear"); no
    shields.
- **Elves** ("beautiful and strong"): Noldo (Noldor), Sinda (Sindar),
  Silvan. No looks on MUME's pages.
  - Noldo (T): tall, dark hair, grey eyes; gem-smiths. Bonus: senses magic.
  - Sinda (T): the Grey Elves. Bonus: repairs armour, leadership, eagles.
  - Silvan (T): wood-elves of Mirkwood and Lórien. Bonus: missiles,
    wilderness, tracking in forests.
- **Half-elves**: no subraces; "resemble Men more than Elves".
- **Hobbits**: about half a man's size, leathery furry feet, no shoes.
  - Fallohide: "taller, slimmer, fairer", the rarest; up to four feet.
  - Harfoot: "shorter, smaller", browner skin, no beards. Bonus: scouting.
  - Stoor: the heftiest, the only breed with facial hair. Bonus: swimming.
- **Dwarves** (houses): Longbeard, Firebeard, Broadbeam. MUME gives only
  bonuses (Longbeard: hard to faze, finds riches, mining; Firebeard: bash,
  anger on criticals, fast sharpening; Broadbeam: calm under fire, parry
  as health drops, armour wears slowly).

## Shadow

- **Orcs** (strong, see in the dark, hurt by sunlight): Tarkhnarb
  (Uruk-Tarkhnarb, Goblin Gate, tunnel-born), Morruhk (Uruk-Morruhk,
  western Misty Mountains: "ritualistic scarification marks", called
  "snufflers", wargs).
- **Zaugurz** (Uruk-Zaugurz): an orc tribe of the snowy far north,
  hostile to both sides; fearless, bloodlust, no casting or riding.
- **Trolls** (huge, bulky, slow, natural hide armour, turn in sunlight):
  Cave ("covered in dark green scales", flat toeless feet), Hill ("hard
  scales", great hammers, burrow), Mountain ("the tallest and wildest").
- **Black Númenórean**: no subraces; "imposing in their height, with
  angular features", proud.
