# The start menu's transitions, before and after stage 74 B (ADR 0164)

Before: `main` at 638df8b2 (the laid-out proxy everywhere). After: `stage-74b` (the proxy in pieces on the compositor on a phone or a tablet, laid out on a desktop; Scripts' syntax check after the page has settled). Both builds measured in the same sitting, 2026-11-19, two bench processes side by side on an otherwise idle machine, 3 rounds each on fresh contexts; `bench/transition-bench.ts`, headless Chromium with this machine's GPU. Full tables: `transitions-74-before.md`, `transitions-74-after.md`.

Each cell is `before → after`, the median over the rounds with (min–max). `click frame`: ms from the tap to the first frame after it. Frame intervals are the page's animation frames over the 1.4 s from the tap (16.7 ms is a 60 Hz frame). `main thread`: throttled time. `GPU process`: this machine's GPU, not throttled: a measure of the work asked for, not of a phone's time.

What the numbers say:

- **Scripts' first visit**: the 180–320 ms frame mid-plan (45 ms on an unthrottled desktop) is gone on every device (the Lua runtime's first compile now waits for the page to settle).
- **Phone and tablet (the pieces)**: frame intervals as before (clean in both builds on this GPU); the GPU process's time over a transition down 30–45 %; with software raster the raster asked for falls to a fifth on the phone and an eighth on the tablet (below). The click frame is 5–25 ms longer under the 4–6× throttle (the pieces' thirteen animations are set up in it).
- **Desktop (unchanged path)**: as before but for Scripts.
- **A blocked main thread** (below): the laid-out proxy stands still for the block, the pieces fly through it.
